## What I built

I was the backend and AI engineer. I built Wazi with Chris Waweru Gichohi, frontend engineer and solutions architect.

## How it works

A retrieval-augmented generation pipeline runs over official county budget and audit documents. Answers come strictly from those documents, in plain language, and Wazi never invents figures. Every answer cites its source. Wazi also has a WhatsApp-style chat channel, a community verification signal for disputed project claims, and a Streamlit admin dashboard.

## Result

Finalist in the Responsible Computing Buildathon (Mozilla Foundation × KamiLimu), July 2026. Piloted for Nakuru County.
